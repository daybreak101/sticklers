2. Combos
Treat combos as a Menu Item
{
  "name": "Combo #1",
  "basePrice": 12.99,
  "type": "combo",
  "comboGroups": ["combo_sandwich", "combo_side", "combo_drink"]
}

comboGroups collection
comboGroup/{groupId}
